Institution Profile

Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda College

The Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda College is a unit of Ramakrishna Mission Vidyapith, Chennai (a branch center of Ramakrishna Mission, Belur Math, West Bengal). It occupies a place of pre-eminence among premier educational institutions in India.

Imbued with a sacred ambience, the institution has been endeavouring for more than 75 years to impart value-based, man-making, and character-building education as envisaged by Swami Vivekananda — nurturing intellectual, moral, and spiritual excellence.
